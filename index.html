Frequently asked

Is DiaryLog a treatment? No. It is a diary card, the tracking sheet used in Dialectical Behavior Therapy, to support the work you do with your therapist or skills group. It doesn't replace professional care, and it isn't a crisis service.

Who can see my diary? Only you. Your entries stay on your phone. DiaryLog has no account and never connects to the internet. You can lock it with Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or your passcode (Settings, App lock).

Can I fill in a day I missed? Yes. Use the arrows or tap the date on the Today screen, or tap a day on the Week screen.

What does the free version include? Everything you need every day: the full card (urges, emotions, skills, the skills-use scale, medication, notes), the week view, the skills library, Help now, the daily reminder, app lock, and backup. DiaryLog Pro, a single purchase, adds sharing the card as a PDF (1, 2 or 4 weeks) and CSV, trends over 30 or 90 days, and your own targets, emotions and skills.

Is there a subscription? No. DiaryLog Pro is a single purchase. Restore it on a new phone from Settings.

How do I move my diary to a new phone? Settings, Back up saves a file with your whole diary; send it to yourself, then use Settings, Restore on the new phone.

Where do the skill descriptions come from? They are short reminders in our own words. Learn and practise the skills with your therapist or skills group.

Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026. DiaryLog is published by Eastframe Technology LLC.

What we collect: nothing. DiaryLog has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting service. It never connects to the internet and sends no data to us or anyone else.

Your diary (ratings, skills, medication answers and notes) is stored only on your device, until you delete it or uninstall the app. Backups and exports are files you create and send where you choose, through the share sheet.

App lock uses your device's Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or passcode. The check happens in the operating system; DiaryLog never sees your biometric data or passcode.

Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We do not see your payment details.

Third parties. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics code. The only external pages it opens are this one and findahelpline.com, and only when you tap them.

Children. DiaryLog is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
